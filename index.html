Program execution | Infrastructure | Reliability

25-Station GNSS Network

I managed a $1M atmospheric monitoring program from site partnerships, procurement, and vendor evaluation through field deployment and data operations. By coordinating a 20-member cross-functional group and redesigning transmission around store-and-forward redundancy, we delivered the network 10% under budget and reduced real-time data gaps from 50% to 2% without losing measurements during temporary outages.

AI workflow | Governance | Human review

Nora AI Assistant

I designed Nora as a governed workflow rather than a single prompt. Retrieval, evidence assessment, response generation, validation, refusal, and human-review routing are separated so teams can locate failure modes, inspect provenance, and decide when automation should stop. The public repository makes both the architecture and its boundaries open to review.

How I solve problems

Make the decision clear, then make action possible.

This is the operating method connecting infrastructure, analytics, strategy, and product work across very different domains.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    Clarify what must be decided, by whom, and which uncertainty is preventing action.

  2. 02

    Interrogate the evidence

    Test what the data support and expose gaps, assumptions, contradictions, and failure modes.

  3. 03

    Design the operating path

    Map owners, dependencies, incentives, handoffs, risks, and the workflow required to deliver.

  4. 04

    Make it usable

    Turn the answer into a model, process, program, product, protocol, or decision workflow others can use.
