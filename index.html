Operational decision flow

Weather intelligence | Program leadership

ISRO Storm Nowcasting

I led the operating layer around an AI-driven storm nowcasting capability: aligning scientists, engineers, and mission stakeholders; defining how performance should be evaluated; and translating model output into guidance for time-sensitive launch reviews. The evaluated system reached 97.5% accuracy with a 5% false-alarm rate and extended useful warning horizons from 15 minutes to two hours.

Research infrastructure

25-Station GNSS Network

I managed a $1M atmospheric monitoring program from site partnerships, procurement, and vendor evaluation through field deployment and data operations. By coordinating a 20-member cross-functional group and redesigning transmission around store-and-forward redundancy, we delivered the network 10% under budget and reduced data gaps from 50% to 2%.

Clean energy | Commercialization

GreenPack Energy Retrofit

I built a five-year operating and financial model spanning more than 80 retrofit categories, using pricing and sensitivity analysis to connect homeowner value, sponsor economics, ESG reporting, and execution capacity. The model helped leadership evaluate growth from 200 to 900 homes annually and prioritize 30 high-fit partners from 103 candidates.

AI product | Governance

Nora AI Assistant

I designed Nora as a governed workflow rather than a single prompt. Retrieval, evidence assessment, response generation, validation, refusal, and human-review routing are separated so teams can locate failure modes, inspect provenance, and decide when automation should stop. The public repository makes both the architecture and its boundaries open to review.
