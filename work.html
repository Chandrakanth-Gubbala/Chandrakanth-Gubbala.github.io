Weather & Research | Technical build

Diurnal IWV Variation

Role: Researcher and analyst. Averaged observations from seven GNSS receivers and applied harmonic analysis plus Fisher's significance testing to three years of 15-minute integrated-water-vapor data, separating dominant 24-hour behavior from weaker 12-hour structure.

Outcome: The diurnal harmonic was significant 93% of the time, and the analysis linked seasonal and day-to-day phase changes primarily to vertically integrated moisture-flux convergence. Public code reproduces the core workflow.

Weather & Research | Validation

INSAT-3D / GNSS Water Vapor Validation

Role: Researcher and validation analyst. Built the spatial and temporal matching workflow used to compare eight years of INSAT-3D total-column water vapor with ground-based GNSS observations and GMI-derived estimates across diurnal, monthly, and seasonal scales.

Outcome: The study isolated when the satellite product reproduced variability, where bias and RMSE changed by region and season, and why radiance validation remained important. Public code documents the core quality-control and comparison method.
